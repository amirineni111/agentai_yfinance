## 🎯 Trading Strategy Framework

### **Beginner Strategy** (2-3 Indicators)
1. Check 200 SMA for trend direction
2. Use RSI for entry timing (30/70 levels)
3. Confirm with MACD crossovers

### **Intermediate Strategy** (3-4 Indicators)
1. Trend: SMA 50/100/200 alignment
2. Momentum: RSI + MACD confirmation
3. Volatility: Bollinger Band positions
4. Risk: ATR for position sizing

### **Advanced Strategy** (All 5 Indicators)
1. **Trend Filter**: Multiple moving average analysis
2. **Momentum Confirmation**: RSI + MACD agreement
3. **Volatility Analysis**: Bollinger Band squeeze/expansion
4. **Risk Management**: ATR-based stops and sizing
5. **Signal Confluence**: Multiple indicator agreement
